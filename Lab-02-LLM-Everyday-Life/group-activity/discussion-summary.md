# Group 4 – Discussion Summary

## Ideas considered

Our first idea was a smartwatch-style device that would track how much a student drinks and remind them during the day.

## How we used an LLM

We used Claude as our design partner while developing the concept.

## Final decision

As the discussion went on, the group agreed that a timetable-aware phone app paired with a smart bottle cap was the best way to meet the needs of this specific problem:

- Students already carry a phone and a water bottle, so they don't need to buy or wear a new device.
- The cap counts bottle openings, so drinking is tracked directly instead of being estimated.
- Linking reminders to the class timetable means nudges arrive at breaks, not in the middle of lectures, so students are less likely to mute them.
- An LLM handles the flexible parts: reading the timetable, understanding schedule changes written in plain words, and writing personalised reminders.
